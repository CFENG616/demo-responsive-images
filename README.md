# Leaf & Pot

Houseplants and handmade pots for small spaces


## Small Plants, Big Personality

Succulents and cacti are the easiest way to bring some green into a small apartment. They need bright light, very little water, and almost no attention.

Every plant comes potted in a speckled concrete pot made by a local maker, so it's ready for your desk or windowsill the day you bring it home.
